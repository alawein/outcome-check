# Protocol only: human evidence review

No participants, human labels, agreement scores, or study outcomes exist in this
release. This protocol describes a future optional study, not completed work.

Recruit evaluators with relevant domain knowledge under an approved study and
consent process. Give each independent reviewer the same explicit goal, supplied
baseline/final observations, clock/subject metadata, and source provenance, without
model or upstream reward labels. Ask them to record eligibility, observed change,
predicate outcome, and uncertainty separately. Never ask reviewers to infer a
missing full database or treat a signature as truth.

Pre-register sample selection by trajectory/task, eligibility exclusions, label
rubric, adjudication procedure, participant count, and agreement statistic before
collection. Include controlled no-op/stale/wrong-subject/timezone/wrong-state cases
and legitimate unchanged-goal controls. Keep independent first-pass labels and
adjudicated labels distinct; protect participant identifiers and withdraw consent
where required. Report missing evidence, disagreements, and rubric revisions.
Do not calculate confidence intervals as though correlated fault variants were
independent people or tasks. Publish only legally reusable observations and
consented labels under separately approved publication scope.
